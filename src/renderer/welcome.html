Braiwser
Annotation-first inspector browser

Review any web project,
click to capture, ship to your agent.

Open a local project or any URL, point at what needs work, leave precise notes, audit accessibility, record journeys as tests — then export a clean review doc, a ready-to-run agent prompt, or a Playwright spec.

1
Open a projectHit File or Folder to load your project’s index.html, or type any URL in the bar.
2
Inspect & click⌘⇧E Inspect, hover to highlight, click to capture the selector, classes and text.
3
Draw or rearrange⌘⇧D circles a region. ⌘⇧M edits the layout live and captures the exact CSS.
4
Audit the page⌘⇧U runs a local contrast / alt-text / label / tap-target scan. Promote any finding into a note.
5
Check every breakpointSwitch the device viewport to review mobile, tablet and desktop — notes remember the size they were taken at.
6
Record & replay⌘⇧J records a journey, assertions turn it into a smoke test, and it exports as a Playwright spec.
7
Hand it offExport Markdown or an agent prompt, or run your coding agent on the review right here with ⌘⇧H.
